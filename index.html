The gap

The bit nobody’s job it is to care about

When an animal is killed on a road in Scotland or elsewhere in the UK, there is usually no dedicated, humane, timely response. Most of what exists is maintenance — and maintenance isn’t mercy.

wildlife deaths in one year of FOI-based highway data for parts of the UK network — only a thin slice of what occurs

Prevent
